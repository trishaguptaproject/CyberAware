Think Before You Click

  • Protect passwords and OTPs
  • Verify links before opening
  • Never trust urgent payment demands
  • Report cybercrime promptly

Know the Common Cyber Threats

Understanding common scams is the first step towards preventing them.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages, and websites designed to steal passwords or financial information.

📱

UPI and Payment Scams

Fraudsters may trick you into approving a payment or sharing a UPI PIN or OTP.

🎭

Identity Theft

Someone uses your personal information without permission to commit fraud.

📞

Fake Calls

Scammers impersonate bank staff, officials, or customer support to create panic.

🔓

Weak Passwords

Simple or reused passwords can make accounts easier to compromise.

🦠

Malicious Downloads

Untrusted apps and attachments can install malware or steal data.

Simple Ways to Stay Safe

Build safer digital habits every day.

🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords and a password manager where possible.

🔐

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra security step to important accounts.

🔗

Check Links Carefully

Verify the sender and website address before clicking.

💳

Never Share OTPs or PINs

Never disclose your OTP, UPI PIN, or banking password to callers.

⬆️

Update Your Devices

Install operating system and app updates from trusted sources.

📶

Use Public Wi-Fi Carefully

Avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted networks.
