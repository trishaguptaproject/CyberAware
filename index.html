KNOW THE RISKS

Common Cyber Threats

Learn how online criminals try to trick people and steal information or money.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to steal your login details or financial information.

Check links
💳

UPI & Payment Scams

Fraudsters may trick you into approving a payment or sharing an OTP, PIN, or payment details.

Never share your PIN
🔑

Password Attacks

Weak or reused passwords can allow someone to access multiple accounts.

Use unique passwords
🦠

Malware

Harmful software may steal data, monitor activity, or damage your device.

Download carefully
👤

Identity Theft

Someone may misuse personal details, documents, or account information without permission.

Limit what you share
💬

Cyberbullying

Harassment, threats, or humiliating content shared through digital platforms.

Save evidence and report
BUILD SAFE HABITS

Simple Ways to Stay Safe

Small habits can make a big difference to your online safety.

01

Use strong passwords

Use long, unique passwords for each account and consider a password manager.

02

Enable two-factor authentication

Add another verification step to important accounts whenever available.

03

Never share OTPs or PINs

Banks and legitimate support staff should not ask you to reveal your UPI PIN or password.

04

Verify before clicking

Check website addresses and confirm unexpected requests through a trusted channel.

05

Update your devices

Install operating-system and app updates from official sources.

06

Think before sharing

Avoid posting sensitive personal details, travel plans, or private documents publicly.

Where to Get Help in India

If a cyber incident happens, act quickly and use official channels.

1930

Financial Cyber Fraud Helpline

If you suspect online financial fraud, contact your bank or payment provider immediately and call 1930 promptly.
